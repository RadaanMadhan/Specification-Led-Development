    rationale: Removing this fact allows two distinct Access events to share a single AuditEntry, which models an audit overwrite (mutation) and breaks the one-audit-per-access invariant required by FR-013 and FR-015. The injection forces exactly this scenario.

  - MUTATE F_CareTeamGating  → runs/C-L3/mutated_F_CareTeamGating.als
