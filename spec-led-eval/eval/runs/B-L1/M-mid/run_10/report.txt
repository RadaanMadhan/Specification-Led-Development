      - FR_011_ClosedTaskEditRule: PASS (vacuous?)
      - ValidationBeforeMutation: PASS (vacuous?)

    rationale: Clearing F_PermanentDeletion allows a LiveTask to share its taskRef with a TaskDeletion loggedRef, meaning a 'deleted' task still appears as a live task — violating the permanent-deletion invariant.
