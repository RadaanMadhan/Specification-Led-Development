  - MUTATE F_AppendOnlyAuditEntries  → runs/A-L3/mutated_F_AppendOnlyAuditEntries.als
    rationale: Clearing this fact removes the uniqueness constraint on (eApp, ePrev, eNew) tuples. The injected violation forces two distinct AuditEntry atoms with identical (app, prev, new) values to co-exist, representing a 'duplicate' (i.e., mutated/overwritten) audit record. Both AppendOnly and FR_018_AuditImmutable assert this cannot happen.
    expected to FAIL: AppendOnly, FR_018_AuditImmutable
      - AppendOnly: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_018_AuditImmutable: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_NoSelfApproval  → runs/A-L3/mutated_F_NoSelfApproval.als
    rationale: Clearing this fact removes the guard preventing an applicant from being the actor on a non-initial audit entry for their own application. The injection forces exactly this situation: an audit entry with a non-null prev where the actor equals the application's applicant.
    expected to FAIL: NoSelfMutation, FR_013_NoSelfApproval
      - NoSelfMutation: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_013_NoSelfApproval: PASS (vacuous?)

  - MUTATE F_NoInfoLeakageOnFailedRead  → runs/A-L3/mutated_F_NoInfoLeakageOnFailedRead.als
    rationale: Clearing this fact allows a failed GET operation to carry a non-null oApp link, meaning the system reveals the existence of the application even in a denied response. The injection forces a failed GET to reference an existing application, which is exactly what the byte-equivalence invariant prohibits.
    expected to FAIL: NoInformationLeakage, FR_020_ByteEquivalentUnauthorized
      - NoInformationLeakage: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_020_ByteEquivalentUnauthorized: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_AuditEndpointAuditorOnly  → runs/A-L3/mutated_F_AuditEndpointAuditorOnly.als
    rationale: Clearing this fact removes the constraint that only RAuditor callers may succeed at GetApplicationAudit. The injection forces a succeeded GetApplicationAudit operation by an ROfficer caller, violating the auditor-only access rule for the audit endpoint.
    expected to FAIL: FR_023_AuditEndpointAuditorOnly
      - FR_023_AuditEndpointAuditorOnly: PASS (vacuous?)
