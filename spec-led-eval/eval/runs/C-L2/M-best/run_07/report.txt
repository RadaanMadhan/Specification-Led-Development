  Strategy: replace each named fact's body with `{}` (no constraint),
  then re-run Alloy. Each listed assertion should now FAIL on the

  - MUTATE F_LeastPrivilegeAllowedMatrix  → runs/C-L2/mutated_F_LeastPrivilegeAllowedMatrix.als
    rationale: Clearing the fact removes the structural rule that permitted operations must lie in the documented permission matrix. The injection materialises a Doctor calling the administrator-only OpListAudit endpoint and succeeding, which is precisely the LeastPrivilege violation. The matrix from F_PermissionMatrix remains intact, so the LeastPrivilege check directly sees the offending cell.
    expected to FAIL: LeastPrivilege, PermissionGrounding
      - LeastPrivilege: FAIL (expected)
      - PermissionGrounding: PASS (vacuous?)

  - MUTATE F_AppendOnlyAudit  → runs/C-L2/mutated_F_AppendOnlyAudit.als
    rationale: Clearing the fact removes the no-duplicate-audit constraint. The injection forces two distinct AuditEntry atoms to point at the same Operation, which is the canonical append-only / immutability violation (one operation, two records of it — i.e., a mutation or duplicate).
    expected to FAIL: AppendOnly, FR_014_AuditImmutable
      - AppendOnly: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_014_AuditImmutable: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_AuditCompleteness  → runs/C-L2/mutated_F_AuditCompleteness.als
    rationale: Clearing the fact removes the always-on audit constraint. The injection forces the existence of an Operation that no AuditEntry references, which is exactly an unaudited access — the FR-012 violation.
    expected to FAIL: AuditCompleteness, FR_012_AuditEveryAttempt
      - AuditCompleteness: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_012_AuditEveryAttempt: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_CareTeamGating  → runs/C-L2/mutated_F_CareTeamGating.als
    rationale: Clearing the fact removes the care-team membership precondition for clinical reads. The injection forces a permitted record-read by a clinician where no active care-team membership links the caller to the patient — the central FR-004 violation.
    expected to FAIL: OwnershipBasedAccess, FR_004_CareTeamGating
      - OwnershipBasedAccess: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_004_CareTeamGating: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_AttributionCorrectness  → runs/C-L2/mutated_F_AttributionCorrectness.als
    rationale: Clearing the fact removes the requirement that audit entries record the actual caller and their role. The injection forces an AuditEntry whose recordedUser differs from the operation's caller, which is direct misattribution — the FR-013 invariant violation.
    expected to FAIL: AttributionCorrectness, FR_013_AuditFields
      - AttributionCorrectness: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_013_AuditFields: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_ByteEquivalentDenial  → runs/C-L2/mutated_F_ByteEquivalentDenial.als
