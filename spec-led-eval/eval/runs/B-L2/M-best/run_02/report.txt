  Mutation tests  (assertion-strength validation)

  Strategy: replace each named fact's body with `{}` (no constraint),
  then re-run Alloy. Each listed assertion should now FAIL on the
  mutated model. If it still PASSes, the assertion is vacuous and
  must be strengthened.

  - MUTATE F_AppendOnlyAuditEntries  → runs/B-L2/mutated_F_AppendOnlyAuditEntries.als
    rationale: Removing the append-only fact lets the model invent two distinct operations producing the same AuditEntry — i.e., an audit row has been overwritten. The injection forces such a sharing to exist, which is exactly what the AppendOnly assertion is supposed to catch.
    expected to FAIL: AppendOnly, FR_016_AuditAppendOnly
      - AppendOnly: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_016_AuditAppendOnly: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_OwnershipBasedEditDelete  → runs/B-L2/mutated_F_OwnershipBasedEditDelete.als
    rationale: Removing the ownership-conditional fact lets a member successfully PATCH a task they do not own. The injection materialises that exact escalation, which the OwnershipBasedAccess assertion must reject.
    expected to FAIL: OwnershipBasedAccess, FR_008_009_010_OwnerOrAdminEditDelete, LeastPrivilege
      - OwnershipBasedAccess: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_008_009_010_OwnerOrAdminEditDelete: FAIL (expected)
      - LeastPrivilege: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_CrossTeamIsolation  → runs/B-L2/mutated_F_CrossTeamIsolation.als
    rationale: Removing the cross-team fact lets a caller succeed on a task that belongs to a different team than their X-Team-Id. The injection forces a successful cross-tenant access, which is exactly the SaaS-multi-tenancy leak NoInformationLeakage forbids.
    expected to FAIL: NoInformationLeakage, FR_011_014_CrossTeamIsolation
      - NoInformationLeakage: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_011_014_CrossTeamIsolation: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_AuthRequired  → runs/B-L2/mutated_F_AuthRequired.als
    rationale: Removing the auth-boundary fact lets an unauthenticated operation produce any outcome other than UnauthenticatedOutcome (e.g., NotFound or ValidationError). The injection forces such a non-Unauth outcome to exist with no caller, which the AuthRequiredEverywhere assertion is meant to forbid.
    expected to FAIL: AuthRequiredEverywhere, FR_001_AuthRequired
      - AuthRequiredEverywhere: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_001_AuthRequired: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_PermissionMatrix  → runs/B-L2/mutated_F_PermissionMatrix.als
    rationale: Clearing the permission-matrix fact leaves PermMatrix.Allowed unconstrained. The injection deletes the MemberRole→GetAudit cell, breaking FR-017 (members must be able to read audit) and PermissionCompleteness (every documented cell must be present).
    expected to FAIL: PermissionCompleteness, FR_017_AuditReadableByMember, PermissionGrounding
      - PermissionCompleteness: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_017_AuditReadableByMember: FAIL (expected)
      - PermissionGrounding: PASS (vacuous?)
