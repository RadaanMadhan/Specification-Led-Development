  - MUTATE F_AppendOnlyDeletionLog  → runs/B-L1/mutated_F_AppendOnlyDeletionLog.als
    rationale: F_AppendOnlyDeletionLog is the sole guard against two TaskDeletion rows for the same task. Clearing it and forcing duplicates exposes any model that secretly allowed re-deletion.
    expected to FAIL: AppendOnly, FR_018_DeletionLogAppendOnly

  - MUTATE F_DeletionLogPerDelete  → runs/B-L1/mutated_F_DeletionLogPerDelete.als

    expected to FAIL: AuditCompleteness, FR_012_DeletionRecorded
      - AuditCompleteness: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_012_DeletionRecorded: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_DeletionLogPerDelete  → runs/B-L1/mutated_F_DeletionLogPerDelete.als

    expected to FAIL: AttributionCorrectness, FR_018_DeletionLogAppendOnly
      - AttributionCorrectness: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_018_DeletionLogAppendOnly: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_ClosedTaskRule  → runs/B-L1/mutated_F_ClosedTaskRule.als
