    rationale: Clearing F_LeastPrivilege removes the constraint that a successful operation's caller must hold a role with permission for the endpoint. The injection forces a successful GetAudit by a caller with only the Applicant role, which the LeastPrivilege assertion is designed to forbid.

    rationale: Clearing F_AppendOnlyAuditEntries removes the uniqueness-per-transition constraint. The injection introduces two distinct AuditEntries describing the same (app, prev, new) slot — exactly the duplication that append-only forbids.
    expected to FAIL: AppendOnly, FR_018_AppendOnly

      - FR_018_AppendOnly: FAIL (expected)
