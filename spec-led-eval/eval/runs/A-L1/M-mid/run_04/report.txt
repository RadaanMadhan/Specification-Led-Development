The injection forces a forbidden cell into Allowed.
    expected to FAIL: LeastPrivilege, PermissionCompleteness, FR_015_RoleSeparation
      - LeastPrivilege: PASS (vacuous?)

      - FR_015_RoleSeparation: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_AppendOnlyAuditEvents  → runs/A-L1/mutated_F_AppendOnlyAuditEvents.als
    rationale: Clearing F_AppendOnlyAuditEvents removes the constraint that decided applications retain their submission events. The injection forces a decided application with no EvtSubmitted event, directly violating AppendOnly and FR_016_AuditTrailComplete.
    expected to FAIL: AppendOnly, FR_016_AuditTrailComplete, FR_017_NoApplicationDeletion
      - AppendOnly: PASS (vacuous?)

      - FR_017_NoApplicationDeletion: PASS (vacuous?)
