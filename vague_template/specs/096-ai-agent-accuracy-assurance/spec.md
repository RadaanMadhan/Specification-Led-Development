# Feature Specification: AI Agent Accuracy Assurance

**Feature Branch**: `096-ai-agent-accuracy-assurance`  
**Created**: 20/05/2026  
**Status**: Draft  
**Input**: Ensure our AI agents are highly accurate and don't make mistakes when talking to customers.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Quality Validation of AI Responses (Priority: P1)

As a customer, I want to receive accurate and reliable information from the AI agent, so I can trust the service and get the right help without frustration.

**Why this priority**: Directly addresses the core requirement of agent accuracy and preventing mistakes.

**Independent Test**: AI agent processes a customer query; if accuracy is deemed low by the validation component, the response is blocked or flagged for review before reaching the customer.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a customer query is received, **When** the AI agent generates a response, **Then** the validation component must assess the response for factual accuracy and relevance.
2. **Given** an AI response is generated, **When** the response is determined to have high accuracy, **Then** it is presented to the customer.
3. **Given** an AI response is generated, **When** the response is determined to have low accuracy, **Then** the response is intercepted and replaced with a default "I need assistance" message.

---

### User Story 2 - Human Supervisor Review (Priority: P2)

As a human supervisor, I want to review flagged low-accuracy AI responses, so I can understand why the AI is making mistakes and provide corrections.

**Why this priority**: Enables continuous improvement of the AI agent based on real-world errors.

**Independent Test**: A supervisor can access a queue of flagged responses and approve or reject them.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an AI response has been flagged as low-accuracy, **When** the supervisor accesses the review queue, **Then** they can see the original customer query and the AI's generated response.
2. **Given** a flagged response is in review, **When** the supervisor provides feedback, **Then** the AI agent's knowledge base is updated to prevent similar errors.

---

### User Story 3 - Accuracy Performance Reporting (Priority: P3)

As a business manager, I want to monitor the accuracy of our AI agents through periodic reports, so I can make informed decisions about agent configuration and training needs.

**Why this priority**: Essential for long-term governance and identifying trends.

**Independent Test**: A report is generated showing accuracy rates over a set time period.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the agent is in operation, **When** a reporting period ends, **Then** the system generates an accuracy report summary.

---

### Edge Cases

- What happens when the AI agent is absolutely certain, but still factually wrong?
- How does the system handle high-volume query periods without causing validation delays?
- What happens when the human supervisor queue reaches capacity?

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: System MUST validate all AI-generated responses for accuracy before delivery to the customer.
- **FR-002**: System MUST flag AI responses as "low-accuracy" based on predefined factual or guideline deviation thresholds.
- **FR-003**: System MUST provide a mechanism for human supervisors to review and provide feedback on flagged responses.
- **FR-004**: System MUST maintain a historical record of all customer queries and AI responses for audit and training.
- **FR-005**: System MUST present a consistent, user-friendly fallback message when a response is blocked.

### Key Entities

- **Customer Query**: The input provided by the customer.
- **AI Response**: The tentative answer generated by the agent.
- **Validation Result**: An assessment (e.g., accuracy score, flag status) of the response.
- **Review Feedback**: Feedback provided by a human supervisor to correct or approve an AI response.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: The AI agent maintains a hallucination rate of less than 0.1% per 10,000 queries.
- **SC-002**: 100% of responses flagged as low-accuracy are prevented from being shown to the customer.
- **SC-003**: 95% of customer interactions receive a rating of "Helpful" in post-interaction surveys.
- **SC-004**: Human review of flagged items is completed within 4 hours of flagging.

## Assumptions

- There exists an underlying factual knowledge base that the validation component can use.
- The AI agent's responses can be intercepted and held before being shown to the customer.
- Human supervisors are available to perform reviews during business hours.

## Formal Requirements & Business KPI Mapping

```alloy
// Simplified Alloy representation of accuracy constraints
sig Query {}
sig Response {
    generatedBy: Agent,
    isAccurate: one Bool
}
sig Agent {}

pred AccuracyConstraint(r: Response) {
    // A response shown to a customer must be accurate
    r.isAccurate = True
}

assert AccuracyGuaranteed {
    all r: Response | r.isAccurate = True
}
```
