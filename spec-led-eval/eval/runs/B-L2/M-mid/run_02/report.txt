    rationale: The permission matrix is the single load-bearing fact for all allow/deny cells. Clearing it removes every explicit Allowed cell assignment, so Alloy is free to populate PermMatrix.Allowed arbitrarily. The injection adds unauthenticated and non-member access to PATCH and DELETE, directly violating LeastPrivilege, AuthRequiredEverywhere, and NoInformationLeakage.
    expected to FAIL: LeastPrivilege, FR_001_AuthRequired, FR_009_NonOwnerMemberCannotEditDelete, FR_011_CrossTeamIsolation, NoInformationLeakage, AuthRequiredEverywhere

      - FR_001_AuthRequired: PASS (vacuous?)

      - FR_011_CrossTeamIsolation: PASS (vacuous?)

      - AuthRequiredEverywhere: PASS (vacuous?)

  - MUTATE F_AppendOnlyAuditEntries  → runs/B-L2/mutated_F_AppendOnlyAuditEntries.als
    rationale: Clearing F_AppendOnlyAuditEntries removes the constraint that audit entries for deleted tasks must survive. The injection creates a DeletedTask whose audit entries all reference a different task, simulating orphaned (effectively erased) audit entries for the deleted task — exactly what append-only is meant to prevent.
    expected to FAIL: AppendOnly, FR_016_AuditAppendOnly
      - AppendOnly: PASS (vacuous?)
      - FR_016_AuditAppendOnly: PASS (vacuous?)

  - MUTATE F_AuditCreatedEntry  → runs/B-L2/mutated_F_AuditCreatedEntry.als
    rationale: Clearing F_AuditCreatedEntry allows tasks to exist without a 'created' audit entry. The injection forces a task that has no DescCreated audit entry at all, directly violating AuditCompleteness and FR_015_AuditPerSave.
    expected to FAIL: AuditCompleteness, FR_015_AuditPerSave
      - AuditCompleteness: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_015_AuditPerSave: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_AuditTeamMatchesTask  → runs/B-L2/mutated_F_AuditTeamMatchesTask.als
    rationale: Clearing F_AuditTeamMatchesTask removes the denormalised team-consistency requirement on audit entries. The injection places an audit entry whose ae_team differs from its ae_task's task_team, simulating a cross-team audit record leak that breaks cross-team isolation on the audit endpoint.
    expected to FAIL: AppendOnly, FR_015_AuditPerSave, NoInformationLeakage
      - AppendOnly: PASS (vacuous?)
      - FR_015_AuditPerSave: PASS (vacuous?)
      - NoInformationLeakage: PASS (vacuous?)

  - MUTATE F_AuditActorAttributed  → runs/B-L2/mutated_F_AuditActorAttributed.als
    rationale: Clearing F_AuditActorAttributed removes the requirement that the audit actor holds a team membership matching the recorded role. The injection creates an audit entry whose actor has no membership in the entry's team at all, simulating a misattributed or fabricated audit record.
    expected to FAIL: AttributionCorrectness, FR_015_ActorRoleSnapshotted
      - AttributionCorrectness: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_015_ActorRoleSnapshotted: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_OwnerImmutableViaMatrix  → runs/B-L2/mutated_F_OwnerImmutableViaMatrix.als
