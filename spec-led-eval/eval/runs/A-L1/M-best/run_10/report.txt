    rationale: Clearing the access-control fact lets the model invent a successful operation by a caller whose role does not grant the operation.     expected to FAIL: LeastPrivilege, FR_015_OnlyStaffCanDecide

  - MUTATE F_AuditEventsExist  → runs/A-L1/mutated_F_AuditEventsExist.als
    rationale: Without the audit-events-exist fact, an application can reach Approved status without a corresponding EvApproved entry. The injection forces such a state, so audit-completeness assertions must fail.
    expected to FAIL: AuditCompleteness, FR_016_AuditTrailComplete, FR_014_DecisionEventRecorded
      - AuditCompleteness: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_016_AuditTrailComplete: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_AppendOnlyAuditLog  → runs/A-L1/mutated_F_AppendOnlyAuditLog.als
    rationale: Clearing the append-only fact removes the uniqueness constraint on (application, eventType). The injection forces two distinct audit events with the same application and eventType, which is exactly what AppendOnly forbids.
    expected to FAIL: AppendOnly, FR_017_RetentionAppendOnly
      - AppendOnly: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_017_RetentionAppendOnly: FAIL (expected)
