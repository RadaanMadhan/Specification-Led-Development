  - MUTATE F_CrossTeamIsolation  → runs/B-L2/mutated_F_CrossTeamIsolation.als
    rationale: Removing this fact allows a cross-team operation to succeed (opOutcome = Success) and even produce an audit entry. Both NoInformationLeakage and FR_011_FR_014_CrossTeamIsolation assert that cross-team operations must yield NotFound with no audit entry — without F_CrossTeamIsolation the solver can find a counterexample trivially.
    expected to FAIL: NoInformationLeakage, FR_011_FR_014_CrossTeamIsolation
      - NoInformationLeakage: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_011_FR_014_CrossTeamIsolation: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_AppendOnlyAuditEntries  → runs/B-L2/mutated_F_AppendOnlyAuditEntries.als
    rationale: Removing this fact allows two distinct AuditEntry atoms to share the same (aeTask, aeActor, aeActorRole, aeChangeDesc, aeTeam) tuple, simulating an in-place overwrite. The AppendOnly and FR_016_AuditAppendOnly assertions check for exactly this uniqueness property and will find the counterexample.
    expected to FAIL: AppendOnly, FR_016_AuditAppendOnly
      - AppendOnly: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_016_AuditAppendOnly: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_OwnerOrAdminMutate  → runs/B-L2/mutated_F_OwnerOrAdminMutate.als
    rationale: Removing this fact allows a non-owner MemberRole caller to get Success on PatchTask/DeleteTask against a task they don't own. Both OwnershipBasedAccess and FR_008_FR_009_FR_010_OwnerAdminAccess assert that such an attempt must yield PermDenied, not Success.
    expected to FAIL: OwnershipBasedAccess, FR_008_FR_009_FR_010_OwnerAdminAccess
      - OwnershipBasedAccess: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_008_FR_009_FR_010_OwnerAdminAccess: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_NoAuditOnFailure  → runs/B-L2/mutated_F_NoAuditOnFailure.als
    rationale: Removing this fact lets the solver assign an opAuditEntry to an operation whose outcome is not Success (e.g., ValidationFail or PermDenied). AuditCompleteness asserts that only successful operations produce audit entries, and ValidationBeforeMutation specifically checks that validation failures leave no trace.
    expected to FAIL: AuditCompleteness, ValidationBeforeMutation
      - AuditCompleteness: PASS (vacuous?)
      - ValidationBeforeMutation: PASS (vacuous?)

  - MUTATE F_AttributionCorrectness  → runs/B-L2/mutated_F_AttributionCorrectness.als
    rationale: Removing this fact allows an AuditEntry to record a different actor or role than the operation's actual caller and callerRole — a mis-attribution. Both AttributionCorrectness and FR_015_AuditEntryShape assert that aeActor = opCaller and aeActorRole = opCallerRole for every operation that produces an entry.
    expected to FAIL: AttributionCorrectness, FR_015_AuditEntryShape
      - AttributionCorrectness: FAIL (expected)
      - FR_015_AuditEntryShape: FAIL (expected)

  - MUTATE F_PermissionMatrix  → runs/B-L2/mutated_F_PermissionMatrix.als
    rationale: Clearing the permission-matrix body leaves PermMatrix.Allowed unconstrained.       - PermissionCompleteness: FAIL (expected)
      - PrivilegeMonotonicity: FAIL (expected)
